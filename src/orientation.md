<h1>Before you begin.</h1>
<p>Work in graphite or a single ink. Learn one drawing decision at a time, then use it on something new. Target times are guidance, not deadlines.</p>
<h2>A small working kit</h2>
<ul><li>HB and 2B pencils; 4B is optional.</li><li>A kneaded or soft eraser and a sharpener.</li><li>Plain A4 drawing paper or printed worksheets.</li><li>A ruler for Chapter 10. A black fineliner or dip pen is optional.</li></ul>
<p>Translate ink and black chalk into graphite if you prefer. Leave paper white; you do not need to imitate tinted paper, white highlights, or historical materials.</p>
<h2>The repeating rhythm</h2>
<ol><li>Look for 3–5 minutes: directions, shapes, accents, and repeated marks.</li><li>Map the bounding box, main axis, 3–7 anchors, and large gaps.</li><li>Copy the selected fragment from Reference A.</li><li>Copy a comparable fragment from Reference B.</li><li>Compare concrete choices in the marks.</li><li>Create a new subject using one of those choices.</li><li>Self-check structure first, style second, detail last.</li></ol>
<h2>Print a working packet</h2>
<p>Use “Print / Save PDF”, or your browser’s print command when JavaScript is disabled. Choose A4, portrait, 100% scale, and turn off browser headers and footers. The stylesheet supplies the margins. Enable background graphics for Chapter 10’s faint grid.</p>
<p>Measure the line below: it should be 50 mm. If it differs, turn off “fit to page” and check the scale. Letter paper may reflow; check print preview and the calibration before drawing. Screen sizes are not physical measurements.</p>

<h2>Fifteen minutes. One familiar subject.</h2>
<p>Draw a shoe, plant, or your hand from life. Work as you normally would. Do not study a chapter first. Write the date, subject, and time used above the drawing.</p>
<p class="space-label">Baseline drawing · 180 × 165 mm at 100% print scale</p>

<h3>Keep it for Chapter 20</h3>
<p>After the final chapter, redraw the same subject for 15 minutes. Place both attempts side by side and compare proportion, line choice, value grouping, and unnecessary detail.</p>
<p>Pause for a 20-minute memory review after Chapters 4, 8, 10, 14, and 18. Revisit an earlier creation task on a separate sheet before opening its self-check.</p>
